# Progressive Career

A Driver Career that starts in any season from 2016 to 2026 as a real driver and moves through the real seasons. Each season brings that year's cars, liveries, helmets, team names and line-ups, and from 2026 on the career stays in 2026. Needs Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.13 or later, and Classic Seasons with its 2016 to 2024 year addons.

## Playing it

Career > Driver Career > **Progressive Career** opens the Progressive settings page first:

| Setting | Values |
|---|---|
| Starting season | 2016 to 2026 |
| R&D | **Real life**: each season's car brings that year's pace, the cars develop through the season as they did in real life, and the R&D tab is hidden. **Hybrid**: every team starts each season in its real order and develops through R&D from there. |
| Team moves | **Follow your driver's real career**: you sign for your driver's real team every season, with no contract scenes and no secret meetings. **Choose your team**: the stock offers and negotiations, with the other teams' line-ups fitted around you. |

**CONFIRM** goes on to the season length and Choose Driver, which lists that season's real drivers in their suits, numbers and team badges. Team Select shows the season's cars and team logos.

In Team HQ, the **Progressive Settings** tab changes R&D for the loaded career at any time.

## What changes each season

- Every seat takes the real line-up of the year, fitted around you with as few changes as possible. Mid-season changes (stand-ins and swaps) happen at the matching race, scaled to your calendar's length.
- 2017 to 2025 run with 10 teams and 20 cars; 2016 has Manor and 2026 has Cadillac.
- Standings start each season in the previous real season's final order.
- The Resume Career card shows the career's year.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `native/` | The in-game plugins. Build with `native/build.sh`; host tests run with `native/test/run.sh`. |
| `data/` | Per-year line-ups, cars, looks, pace, development curves, mid-season changes and standings, generated by `tools/gen.py`. |
| `db/`, `erp/`, `lang/` | The career content, the grid and wizard rows, and the text. |

Package with `tools/pack_addon.py --zip addons/progressive <out>`.
