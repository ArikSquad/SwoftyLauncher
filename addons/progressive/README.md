# Progressive Career

A Driver Career that starts in any season from 2016 to 2026 as a real driver and moves through the real seasons. Each season brings that year's cars, liveries, helmets, team names and line-ups, and from 2026 on the career stays in 2026. Needs Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.27 or later, and Classic Seasons with its 2016 to 2024 year addons.

## Playing it

Career > Driver Career > **Progressive Career** opens the Progressive settings page first:

| Setting | Values |
|---|---|
| Starting season | 2016 to 2026 |
| R&D | **Real life**: each season's car brings that year's pace, the cars develop through the season as they did in real life, and the R&D tab is hidden. **Hybrid**: every team starts each season in its real order and develops through R&D from there. |
| Team moves | **Follow your driver's real career**: you sign for your driver's real team every season, with no contract scenes and no secret meetings. **Choose your team**: the stock offers and negotiations, with the other teams' line-ups fitted around you. |

**CONFIRM** goes on to the season length and Choose Driver, which lists that season's real drivers in their suits, numbers and team badges. Team Select shows the season's cars and team logos.

In Team HQ, the **Progressive Settings** tab changes R&D for the loaded career at any time.

## What changes each season

- Every seat takes the real line-up of the year, fitted around you with as few changes as possible. Mid-season changes (stand-ins and swaps) happen at the matching race, scaled to your calendar's length.
- A new season's line-up arrives at the first Team HQ that has the season's race calendar, so after Start Next Season the seats change once the calendar is set.
- 2017 to 2025 run with 10 teams and 20 cars; 2016 has Manor and 2026 has Cadillac.
- Every AI driver takes his ratings of that season (experience, racecraft, awareness, pace, focus and the overall they make), mid-season stand-ins included: EA's launch ratings of each season's game for 2020 to 2026, and for 2016 to 2019 a model of the real results calibrated on them. Your driver starts a new career with his real ratings of the starting season; from there his ratings are his own. The first 2026 season brings back the game's 2026 ratings, and the game's own ratings carry on from there.
- Standings start each season in the previous real season's final order.
- The Resume Career card shows the career's year.
- Race weekend and season titles (the starting grid, session intros, results, loading screens) show the career's year.
- 2016 to 2025 race on the 2025 fuel: the race and qualifying fuel loads and the fuel burn per lap of every track are the game's 2025 numbers, which suit those seasons' 2025 power units. From 2026 on, the game's 2026 numbers apply.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `native/` | The in-game plugins. Build with `native/build.sh`; host tests run with `native/test/run.sh`. |
| `data/` | Per-year line-ups, cars, looks, pace, driver ratings, development curves, mid-season changes and standings, generated by `tools/gen.py` (ratings by `tools/ratings.py` from `official_ratings.json`, `tools/official_ratings.py`, and `ratings_model.json`, `tools/ratings_model.py`); `titles.json`, the stock event and season titles with the year 2026, from `tools/titles.py`. |
| `db/`, `erp/`, `lang/` | The career content, the grid and wizard rows, and the text. |

Package with `tools/pack_addon.py --zip addons/progressive <out>`.
