# wearables: helmets, suits, gloves, looks and team kit

Owner: see `subsystems.tsv`. Prefix `Wear`. Scope: the item rows (`customisation.custom_avatar.{helmets,suits,gloves}`), the driver customisations a driver wears (`entrants.driver_customisations.folder_N.preloaded_driver_customisation_properties_M`, "looks"), the team customisations per season (`entrants.team_customisation.team_customisation_for_year_N.team_customisation_M`, "team kit"), the catalogue build and the driver actor spawner's choice of kit. The entrant record that names a look is `entrants` (EntrantRecord+0x54); the design for the core's appliers is work/std16/RE-WEARABLES.md (gitignored, std16 worktree).

## Load: looks {#looks}
`Wear_Load_DriverCustomisations` (0x142466a10, param 2 = domain: 1 and 2 from the EntrantsSystem constructor 0x142460b90, 0 from `Wear_Load_TeamCustomisations`) walks every `entrants.driver_customisations` folder and its `preloaded_driver_customisation_properties` rows. A row with `available` false is skipped (0x1424670b0). Per row: `helmet` (label to the helmet row's `stat_id`, else `id`), `suit` (`Wear_Resolve_SuitSetup` 0x142490800), `gloves` (`Wear_Resolve_GloveSetup` 0x142490a70), `pose`, `podium_emote`, `victory_vo` (each label to its row's `stat_id`/`id`), `linked_driver_stat_id` (an `entrants.drivers` label to its `stat_id`), then `stat_id`; the record goes into the domain's array (EntrantsSystem+0x298+domain*0x10, indexed by stat id, `Wear_Get_DriverCustomisation_Thunk` 0x14246d940). The record keeps the linked driver at +0x330 (domain byte) / +0x332 (u16 stat id) (`Wear_Serialise_DriverCustomisation` 0x142491d50 writes them as `linked_driver_stat_id`).

`available` is set at boot by `Sess_Apply_ContentAvailability` (0x1413e4cf0): it collects EntrantRecord +0x50/+0x54/+0x58/+0x5c of the available entrant lists and marks rows whose `stat_id` is in the set (driver customisations included, pass at 0x1413eadf9; 0x1413e8d52 is the entrants.selection_domains pass). A look that no available entrant names stays unloaded (L1: the pass that covers driver customisations is read, its table cursor name is stack-built and not decoded yet).

The entrant row's `driver_customisation` label is packed by `Ent_Pack_DriverCustomisation` (0x142453880, entrants lane): it searches the label in every folder's `preloaded_driver_customisation_properties` and returns `stat_id << 16` (domain byte 0), `0x3fff0000` on a miss (found path 0x142453be2..0x142453c0f; the KB proto lacks its second argument, the label in rdx).

## Load: team kit {#team-kit}
`Wear_Load_TeamCustomisations` (0x142485190, EntrantsSystem vtable slot at 0x14578af28) reloads the domain-0 looks, clears the team-kit containers and walks `entrants.team_customisation.team_customisation_for_year` rows: `f1_season` (a `game_setup.seasons` label, resolved to the season id byte by 0x142a0e570), then the nested `team_customisation` rows: `suit`, `gloves`, `linked_team` (`entrants.teams` label to `stat_id << 16`), `is_default_for_team`, `_metadata_`, `stat_id`. Each record goes into the list at EntrantsSystem+0x2f8 (`Wear_Insert_TeamCustomisation` 0x1424746e0). Then each label in `official_driver_customisations` is packed with `Ent_Pack_DriverCustomisation` and inserted into a multimap at EntrantsSystem+0x5e0 (buckets +0x608, mask +0x620, list head +0x5f0) keyed by the team customisation's stat id. A second pass over the year rows reads `isDefaultForYear` and `stat_id` into the per-season default map at EntrantsSystem+0x640 (buckets +0x658, mask +0x670).

Record layout, from the serialiser `Wear_Serialise_TeamCustomisations` (0x1424acf30) and the deserialiser `Wear_Deserialise_TeamCustomisations` (0x1424895f0): suit setup +0x00, glove setup +0x30, season id +0xf8, linked team +0xfa (domain) / +0xfc (stat id), is_default_for_team +0xfe, stat id +0x100 (domain) / +0x102 (id). The spawner copies the suit as +0x10..+0x2c and the gloves as +0x40..+0x5c.

## Pick: what a driver wears {#pick}
`Wear_Spawner_ResolveDriverSetup` (0x1401aae90, log channel DRIVER_ACTOR_SPAWNER, the body of `getCustomisationOverridesForProvidedSeason`) decides the kit for a spawned driver actor from the participant's team, season and driver customisation (the entrant's look):
1. A special-edition livery with a team customisation (`custom_se_liveries.se_livery.team_customisation`): look in that kit's official list for a look whose linked driver is this driver; use it whole, else the kit's suit and gloves ("isn't officially linked to team in the special edition livery").
2. No team: the season's default kit (`isDefaultForYear`).
3. Otherwise "Using season stat ID [%i] to get team customisation for team [%s]": the first record in the list with that linked team and season; else "Looking for customisation for team [%s] with no season stat ID": the first record of that team in any season; else "Team [%s] has no valid customisation, use the provided driver customisation [%s]": the look as the entrant names it (suit and gloves 0xffff: the year default).
4. With a kit found: walk the official multimap for the kit's stat id. If the entrant's look is in it, the look is worn whole. If not, "Current driver customisation id [%s] isn't officially linked to team [%s], use the teams generic customisation": the kit's suit and gloves replace the look's (the helmet stays the look's; L1, read from the copy of +0x10..+0x5c only).

Consequences: a team with no `team_customisation` row in any season wears each driver's own look (Classic Seasons relies on this: its season teams have no kit rows and its looks carry the season suit). A team with kit rows dresses every driver who is not in that kit's official list in the kit's suit and gloves (Progressive appends its looks to the 2026 kit rows for this reason). Callers: 0x1401b705e, 0x1401b727e (driver actor spawner), 0x1428908a8 (frontend scene).

## Items and the catalogue {#catalogue}
`Game_Build_CustomisationCatalogue` (0x1414059c0): per avatar item row it reads `is_official_team_item` and formats `colour_%u_default` (0x1456e2fc1) four times with 0x1401009f0, dereferencing each `Gdb_Get_FieldInt` result without a check (item +0x58, +0x5c, +0x60, +0x64 for helmets). A row missing any of the four crashes boot.

## Items: rows and asset groups {#items}
Item rows (`customisation.custom_avatar.suits.Suit_N`, `gloves.Gloves_N`, `helmets.Helmet_N`) carry `id` (the item id; the catalogue vector index), `asset_group` (`asset_groups/[CHARACTER_PACKAGE]/<folder>/<name>.assetgroup`; the package token resolves to `2025_asset_groups/character_package`), `material_override`, `name_loc_tag`, `nb_colours`, `is_official_team_item`, `outfit`, the four `colour_N_default` keys and per-type model keys: suits `feet_model`, `logos_model_override_pattern` (`[GENDER]` in the path); gloves `logos_model`; helmets `model`, `attachments_model`, `use_attachment_material_from_helmet`. A team's suit and gloves rows share the team livery group (`team_livery/<season>-<team>/<season>-<team>.assetgroup`); a driver's own rows use the driver group (`drivers/male/<season>-<team>_<driver>/<driver>.assetgroup`, helmet in `<driver>_helmet.assetgroup`). A new group is a `group.register` over the base group's erp list plus the new erp (Classic Seasons suits.json); rows then point at it.
