# T012 Does the track acclimatisation guard keep Driver Career practice at an imported community track running when the car leaves the garage?

- Requested by: acclimguard, 2026-10-28
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
With the track acclimatisation guard in core, does a Driver Career practice session at an imported community track (Valencia `mod.hanoi`, Sepang `mod.bahrain24`) get past the moment the car leaves the garage (the 1.5.21 to 1.5.23 crash, `ACCESS_VIOLATION reading 0x4 at F1_25.exe+0x299e59b`, object `track_acclim`) and keep running for a full lap?

## Hypothesis
`Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_CopyTargets` fills the programme's targets (self+0x350..+0x358) from the track's TrackAcclimatisationResource (`g_Game_TrackAcclimatisationResource`, CornerData). An imported track has none, so the vector stays empty and null. `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_Start` returns on an empty vector, so the programme never starts, but the drive-out handler `Game_PracticeProgramme_HandleDriveOut` still calls slot +0xf0, which runs `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_RefreshState` (signature `game.practice_track_acclimatisation_refresh_state`) and faults on entry [0]. The guard returns from the refresh when the vector is empty, so the session goes on with the programme inert (no Track Acclimatisation gates, nothing to score). At a stock track the vector holds targets and the guard calls the game's code, so the programme plays as before.

## Promotes
- `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_RefreshState`, `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_CopyTargets`: L2 -> L3 on the build the run used.
- `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_Start` and `Game_PracticeProgramme_HandleDriveOut`: L1 -> L3 only together with a verify row.
- A crash in 0x14299e3a0..0x14299e8a6 with the guard armed, or any other crash in the programme (0x142998000..0x1429e2000) after the car leaves the garage, demotes the "programme stays inert" claim in the Start notes and keeps the guard off.

## User steps
1. Launch through the launcher with the profile above. At the home screen, check `loader.log` for the guard lines (below).
2. Driver Career: continue or start a career whose next weekend is Valencia (or skip ahead to it). Enter Practice 1 and leave the practice programme choice as it comes (or pick Track Acclimatisation if the menu offers it).
3. Leave the garage and drive one full lap, then return to the garage and leave it a second time.
4. Back to the weekend hub; repeat steps 2 and 3 at Sepang in Practice 2.
5. Control: Practice 1 at a stock track (Bahrain or Spain), pick Track Acclimatisation, drive out and run it for one lap. The gates should show and score as before.
6. Quit from the main menu and keep `loader.log`.

## Expected log lines
- `core mixin set 'swofty_core\.dll \(track acclimatisation guard\)': 1 mixin` → the guard was offered.
- `\[core\] wrap Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_RefreshState at 0x[0-9a-f]+ \(base\+0x299e3a0\) installed` (Steam) or `base\+0x2997720` (EA) → the binding resolved and the hook is in.
- `\[acclim\] track acclimatisation guard armed` → the set installed.
- `\[acclim\] Track Acclimatisation programme 0x[0-9a-f]+ has no targets at this track \(targets 0x0\); refresh skipped` at Valencia and Sepang when the car leaves the garage → the guard met the programme 1.5.21 faulted on. No such line at the stock track in step 5.
Pass rule: steps 2 to 5 finish without a crash, the first three lines are logged, and the Track Acclimatisation gates work at the stock track. Fail rule: any crash with `F1_25.exe+0x299e` or `+0x2997` (EA) in its stack, a Track Acclimatisation that no longer shows at the stock track, or the guard lines missing.
