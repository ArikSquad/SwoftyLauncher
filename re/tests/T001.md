## Question
With B24 installed, does a 2026 Grand Prix select 24 entrants?

## Hypothesis
`research/grid24-cap.md` §2: the selector `Sess_Select_Entrants` computes `limit = min(max_entrants, #candidates)` because `reuse_entrants` is 0. With B24 the f1_2026 list has 24 parsed rows (`_entrant_instancecount_ = 24`) and `max_entrants = 24`, so at `site_Sess_SelectorLimitLoad` (0x1424a30a8) `[rax]` reads 24, `[[rsp+0x60]+0x268]` (EntrantSetupMode.max_entrants) is 24 and the candidate count `[rsp+0x78]` is 24. `Sess_Weekend_Start` then receives 24 entrant pointers (`ep_end - ep_begin = 0xc0`).
