## Hypothesis
`subsystems/wearables.md#looks`. `Wear_Load_DriverCustomisations` skips a row whose `available` is false (0x1424670b0). Every look ships `available` false, so the startup availability pass (`Sess_Apply_ContentAvailability`) must mark the looks that entrants name; a look no available entrant names stays unloaded and `Wear_Get_DriverCustomisation_Thunk` returns null for it. Classic Seasons relies on its looks being named by its entrant rows.

## Promotes
- `Wear_Load_DriverCustomisations`, `Wear_Get_DriverCustomisation_Thunk`, `Sess_Apply_ContentAvailability`: L2 -> L3 for the look availability rule.
- A loaded unnamed look means looks are always available; the design then needs no entrant row to ship a look.
