## Question
From the creation of a new Driver Career to its first race weekend, which callers ask Game_Championship_HasTeam about which team keys?

## Hypothesis
`Game_Championship_HasTeam` (thunk 0x140aea5f0, body `Game_Championship_HasTeam_Body` 0x149423b70) answers from the championship's setup modes' entrant lists. The career's own team check `Game_CareerRegistry_HasTeam` (0x1407c4080) defers to it only when CareerEntity +0x10c8 is set, so in a career with the flag clear the callers are the R&D team models and other championship-level readers, not the entrant validation (`Game_CareerSetup_ValidateEntrantTeams_Thunk`). Every asked key of kind 0 that is one of the career's teams answers true.

## Promotes
- `Game_Championship_HasTeam`, `Game_Championship_HasTeam_Body`: L2 -> L3 (team key in edx, kind in the low byte, stat id in the high word).
- Callers found here get named; a return address right after 0x149a5204e (the validation body) or 0x140c2bbfe (Game_CareerSetup_BuildEntrants), reached through the registry check's tail jump at 0x1407c40c2, shows CareerEntity +0x10c8 set in a career, which changes the participant source in work/std16/RE-CAREER-TEAMS.md.
