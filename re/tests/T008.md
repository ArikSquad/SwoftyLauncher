# T008 drivers.freeAgent.add: does a late Driver Manager driver get a teamless entrant row and a contract that load back, take a race seat and survive a weekend and a season change

- Requested by: ce-lateagent, 2026-10-26
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
Does `drivers.freeAgent.add` give a Driver Manager driver made after a Driver Career started a teamless entrant row and a contract that behave like a pool free agent's: they load back after a save, `drivers.swap` seats him, and the seat survives a weekend advance and a season change?

## Hypothesis
Static RE (protocol/drivers.md, `Op drivers.freeAgent.add`): the setup's entrant vector object is {allocator +0x2d8, begin +0x2e0, end +0x2e8, capacity count +0x2f0} (`Game_CareerSetup_ResetEntrants`, `Sess_Assign_EntrantVector`); every row has a 0x58-byte `DriverContract` slot made by `Career_Rebuild_DriverContracts` and `Career_Load_DriverContracts` (zeroed, allocator at +0x30, driver at +0x50; record and `active` only with has_contract); `Career_Save_DriverContracts` saves every slot; `Ent_Deserialise_EntrantVector` resizes the vector to the saved count; AI rows match by the +0x48 dword alone (`Sess_Match_EntrantIdentity_Thunk`). Not settled statically: an untouched free agent's contract bytes and +0x48 high byte (the op copies them from one and refuses unless his contract record is zero and his history empty), the vector saver, the driver look (+0x54, the op writes 0x3fff0000), and whether the current weekend's line-up copy picks up the row.

## Promotes
- `Sess_Assign_EntrantVector`, `Sess_Match_EntrantIdentity_Thunk` (L2 -> L3 with the run)
- `Career_Rebuild_DriverContracts`, `Career_Push_DriverContractSlot`, `Career_Load_DriverContracts`, `Career_Save_DriverContracts` (L1; their layout claims are confirmed by the reload)
- career inbox items: `CareerEntity` +0x2d8 / +0x2f0, `DriverCareerContractOwner` +0x210 / +0x290 / +0x2a8, `DriverContract` +0x30, size 0x58
- Fail at step 4 or 6 demotes the vector-object and contract-slot claims and the op stays disabled.

## User steps
Use a backed-up Driver Career save started before a Driver Manager driver was added (or start one, then add a driver in Driver Manager and launch again).
1. Load the career to Team HQ. Keep `snapshot.json`: the driver is in `drivers.otherDrivers` with a `dm_` name.
2. On Drivers, open Swap seats and run the add with `dryRun` (the launcher's fake bridge or a manual inbox op `{"op":"drivers.freeAgent.add","args":{"driverStatId":<id>,"dryRun":true}}`). Keep the `template free agent` log line: it is the dump of an untouched never-seated AI free agent's `ordinal_flags` (+0x48, high byte) and his whole `DriverContract` object.
3. Press **Add to line-up as free agent** for the driver. Keep `snapshot.json`: he is in `drivers.drivers` with `team` null, `seat` {hasCar false, position null}, `raceNumber` null, `contract` {teamStatId 0, active false}.
4. Save in Team HQ, quit to the main menu, load the career again. Same snapshot as step 3.
5. **Swap seats**: put him into an AI race seat. Keep the swap log lines and the snapshot (team, car, contract active for that team, a race number). Check the Team HQ grid, standings and driver market.
6. Advance to the next weekend and load into the first session: he drives the car (name and number on the HUD). Save, quit, reload: he keeps the seat.
7. Simulate or play to the season change. Keep the logs. Check who holds the seat afterwards and that the career keeps loading.

## Expected log lines
- `drivers\.freeAgent\.add driver \d+: template free agent \d+ ordinal_flags 0x[0-9a-f]{8} driver_customisation 0x[0-9a-f]{8} contract 0{96}[0-9a-f]{16}0{16}[0-9a-f]{8}0{8}` → the template contract is zero except the allocator (+0x30), history pointers (+0x38..+0x47, zero for never-seated) and driver (+0x50); records the +0x48 high byte
- `drivers\.freeAgent\.add driver \d+: entrant row added \[team 16383 car 0x3fff0000 pos -1, entrant index \d+, copied from free agent \d+\], contract created \(inactive, zero record\); (\d+) -> \d+ rows` → the op wrote and verified both
- `drivers\.swap driver \d+ .* \(Driver Career replace: incoming announced` then `drivers\.swap signed 1/1 seat contract` → step 5
- no `drivers\.freeAgent\.add FAILED` and no crash in steps 3-7
Pass rule: steps 3, 4 and 6 show the row and the contract; step 5 seats him with a contract; step 7 loads. Fail rule: any FAILED line, the row or contract missing after step 4, a crash, or the season change dropping or duplicating a driver (keep the logs).
