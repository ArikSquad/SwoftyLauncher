# T003 Where do the 26-car Grand Prix entrants go between selection and the session?

- Requested by: grid-cap, 2026-10-17
- Status: queued
- Session: the B26g (or B26d) grid run; same launch, no extra steps

## Question
With B26g installed, how many entrants does the selector produce for a 2026 Grand Prix, how many reach `Weekend::start`, and which ones does the session filter drop?

## Hypothesis
`research/grid24-cap.md#gp-custom-team-mode`:
- The GP 2026 path runs entrant setup mode row 10 `f1_custom_team_2026` (stat 12) through `EntrantSetupModeOverride f1_26 → f1_custom_team_2026`.
- Its pool is list 66: 26 entrants from list 34 via `entrant_list_references`, plus 2 custom-team seats (team `f1_custom_team_26`, stat 488).
- With `max_entrants` 28 the selector keeps all 28.
- The empty custom team is then removed (`remove_team_without_players=True`, parsed in `0x14290bd30`), leaving 26.
- B26c's run showed `limit=24 candidates=28` and 22 on track, i.e. 24 selected minus the 2 custom seats.

## Promotes
- **On pass:** row 10 `max_entrants` and list 66 `entrant_list_references` to L3. The selector-return and Weekend::start filter sites become KB signatures. The second cut gets attributed to the stage where the two `team=0x01e80000` (stat 488) entrants disappear.
- **On a cut not explained by stat 488:** open a follow-up on the stage it happens in.

| hook | site | logs |
|---|---|---|
| `t003.selector_limit` | `0x1424a30a8` (KB `session.selector_limit_load`) | `limit`, `mode_stat` (12 = row 10), `max_entrants`, `max_teams`, `reuse_entrants`, `candidates` |
| `t003.selector_ret` | `0x14245ff4e`, right after `call Sess_Select_Entrants` (`r15` = entrants manager) | `sess_begin`/`sess_end` (post-selection count = (end−begin)/0x88), `cand_begin`/`cand_end` (pool = (end−begin)/0x88), `mode`, `list` |
| `t003.weekend_start` | `0x140af0460` (KB `session.weekend_start`) | `ep_begin=[rdx+8]`, `ep_end=[rdx+0x10]` (count = (end−begin)/8), `filter` (callback at `this+0x208`), `cur_session` |
| `t003.ws_a_seen` | `0x140af0643` (loop A, before the filter call) | each entrant offered: `ent`, `drv`, `team`, `veh`, `pos`, `car` |
| `t003.ws_a_kept` | `0x140af0651` (loop A, kept or no filter) | each entrant kept: `ent`, `team`, `car` |
| `t003.ws_b_verdict` | `0x140af0852` (loop B, previous-session path, after the filter) | `drop` (non-zero = dropped), `ent`, `drv`, `team`, `veh`, `car` |

Field decoding: `team` and `drv` are `stat_id << 16`.

| team | stat | `team` value |
|---|---|---|
| T12 `team12_26` | 500 | `0x01f40000` |
| T13 `team13_26` | 501 | `0x01f50000` |
| custom team `f1_custom_team_26` | 488 | `0x01e80000` |

For B26d, T12/T13 are 223/230 (`0x00df0000`/`0x00e60000`). `car` is the race number (T12 66/65, T13 70/40).

Note: T001's `weekend_start` fields (`[rdx]`, `[rdx+8]`) read the allocator and begin. The `EntrantPointers` begin/end pointers are at `+8`/`+0x10` (Weekend::start decompile), which T003 uses.

## Expected log lines
- `t003.selector_limit … limit=0x1c … mode_stat=0xc … max_entrants=28 … candidates=0x1c`
- `t003.selector_ret … sess_end − sess_begin = 0x1c × 0x88 = 0xee0` (28 selected)
- `t003.weekend_start … ep_end − ep_begin = 0xd0` (26 pointers), or 0xe0 (28) followed by two drops with `team=0x01e80000`
- The `ws_*` lines list 26 kept entrants, including `team=0x01f40000` ×2 and `0x01f50000` ×2.

Pass: 26 on track, and the counts above show where the two custom seats leave. Fail: fewer than 26; the first stage whose count drops below 26, and the `team` values missing there, name the next cut. Inconclusive: no `t003.*` E lines (check the R lines).
