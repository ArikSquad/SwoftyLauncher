# T119 When is a CarLive built and torn down: once per session load, again on session restart, on flashback, on returning to the garage?

## Question
When is a CarLive built and torn down: once per session load, again on session restart, on flashback, on returning to the garage?

## Hypothesis
Car_Chassis_Build (0x144628190) allocates one CarLive (0x9f60) per car when the vehicle entity's physics component initialises, i.e. once per car at session load; Car_Destroy_Live (0x14473cf70) removes it at session unload. A session restart rebuilds every car (new CarLive pointers); a flashback and reset-to-track do not (they go through Car_Systems_Reset_Thunk, see T120). `site_Car_LiveCarBuilt` (sig `car.live_car_built`, rdi = CarLive) fires once per car per build; `site_Car_VehicleTableRemove` (sig `car.vehicle_table_remove`) once per car per teardown.

## User steps
1. Start a Grand Prix weekend practice session (any track, 20+ cars) and wait until the car is in the garage.
2. Drive out, complete one sector, then use one flashback.
3. Restart the session from the pause menu.
4. Quit to the main menu.

## Expected log lines
- `t119.car_built .*id=(\d+).*n=(\d+)` → one line per car after step 1 proves one CarLive per car at load; n should be 23 (the VTF systems).
- no `t119.car_built` lines during step 2 → flashback keeps the cars.
- a second batch of `t119.car_built` lines (with `t119.car_removed` lines before it) after step 3 → restart rebuilds the cars.
- `t119.car_removed` lines at step 4 → teardown at session exit.
Pass rule: the line counts match the car count at steps 1, 3 and 4 and are zero at step 2. Fail rule: any other pattern (record which).
