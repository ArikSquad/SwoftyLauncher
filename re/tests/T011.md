# T011 Does the ERS new-lap guard keep a Time Trial with a Classic Seasons box at Autopolis running past the vehicle_newlap that crashed 1.5.21?

- Requested by: lapguard, 2026-10-28
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
With the ERS new-lap guard in core, does a Time Trial at Autopolis with a Classic Seasons box picked get past its first `vehicle_newlap` (the 1.5.21 crash, `ACCESS_VIOLATION reading 0xe8 at F1_25.exe+0x25180f7`, 0.1 to 0.5 s after `StateLoadSession` exits) and keep running through two flying laps?

## Hypothesis
`Game_ErsSystem_BindVehicles` leaves entry+0x28 at 0 for a participant without a vehicle component, and `Game_ErsSystem_HandleVehicleNewLap` reads through it at `site_Game_ErsNewLap_VehicleLookup` (signature `game.ers_new_lap_vehicle_lookup`). The guard sends such an entry to `site_Game_ErsNewLap_NextVehicle` (`game.ers_new_lap_next_vehicle`), the handler's own path for an entry that does not match, so the handler finishes its search and the session goes on. The car whose lap changed still gets its lap energy counters reset when its entry is bound. Why the first `vehicle_newlap` comes right after the load at a community track and not at stock tracks is not known (see `subsystems/game.md` inbox, 2026-10-28).

## Promotes
- `site_Game_ErsNewLap_VehicleLookup`, `site_Game_ErsNewLap_NextVehicle`, `Game_ErsSystem_HandleVehicleNewLap`, `Game_ErsSystem_BindVehicles`: L2 -> L3 on the build the run used.
- A crash at `F1_25.exe+0x25180f3` or `+0x25180f7`, or any crash inside the handler with the guard armed, demotes the skip claim in the site notes and keeps the guard off.

## User steps
1. Launch through the launcher with the profile above. At the home screen, check `loader.log` for the guard lines (below).
2. Time Trial: pick the Classic Seasons box in the era picker's Addons row, track Autopolis, any car, start the session.
3. Wait at least 5 s after the load screen closes, then drive two full laps across the line.
4. Back to the Time Trial menu; repeat steps 2 and 3 at Sochi.
5. Control: the same Classic Seasons box at a stock track (Suzuka), one lap. Note whether any `[ers] vehicle_newlap` line appears.
6. Quit from the main menu and keep `loader.log`.

## Expected log lines
- `core mixin set 'swofty_core\.dll \(ers new lap guard\)': 1 mixin` → the guard was offered.
- `site site_Game_ErsNewLap_VehicleLookup at 0x[0-9a-f]+ \(base\+0x25180f3\) installed` (Steam) or `base\+0x2511473` (EA) → the site resolved and the hook is in.
- `\[ers\] new-lap guard armed` → the set installed.
- `\[ers\] vehicle_newlap for vehicle [0-9]+: participant [0-9]+ of [0-9]+ has no vehicle` at the Autopolis start → the guard met the participant 1.5.21 faulted on.
Pass rule: steps 2 to 4 finish without a crash and the first three lines are logged. Fail rule: any crash with `F1_25.exe+0x25180f` or `+0x251147` in its stack, or the guard lines missing.
