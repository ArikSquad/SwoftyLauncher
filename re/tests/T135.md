## Question
Does a 12-team career whose teams core career::apply_teams builds from season records start, race a 24-car weekend and cross a season break into a season with a closed slot without a crash?

## Hypothesis
Participants are copied once at creation (`Game_CareerSetup_ResetEntrants`, `Game_CareerSetup_BuildEntrants`) from the data group's entrant list, so 12 slot rows in that list give 12 teams with per-team state. A closed slot is Progressive's ten-team year (both seats released teamless through the apply_lineup batch), which already crosses season breaks in game. The season-start work runs at `career.season.start`, after the season has weekends and a current weekend (#421).

## Promotes
- `Game_CareerRegistry_HasTeam`, `Game_CareerSetup_ValidateEntrantTeams_Thunk`: L2 -> L3 (no row of the 12 teams loses its team at creation).
- A crash or a lost team demotes the slot model in RE-CAREER-TEAMS.md for the failing table.
