# T130 At car build, does Car_FrontWing_Init register exactly 11 front wing pieces per car through Car_Register_DetachablePart_Thunk, 3 with the centre predicate, 4 with the right and 4 with the left, and nothing on later Init calls?

- Requested by: car-re, 2026-10-28
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
At car build, does Car_FrontWing_Init register exactly 11 front wing pieces per car through Car_Register_DetachablePart_Thunk, 3 with the centre predicate, 4 with the right and 4 with the left, and nothing on later Init calls?

## Hypothesis
`Car_FrontWing_Init` 0x14465f620 is slot 6 (+0x30 Init) of the F1WingDamage system vtable. `Car_Chassis_Build` calls it with reset 0 at 0x14462973d (return 0x144629740), and `Car_Systems_Reset_Body` calls it again with its reset argument (return 0x1555bdfed). With reset 0 and an empty `CarDamageFrontWing.parts_begin == parts_end` it makes 11 calls to `Car_Register_DetachablePart_Thunk`. Each call creates its main piece through `Car_Add_DetachablePart_Thunk` and then `Car_Create_DetachablePart`, followed by any `<name>_%03d` sub-pieces. Per call site (return address in Init), predicate and k (car.md#parts):

| # | Init return | piece | predicate | k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0x14465f89d | frontspoiler_fm | centre 0x144660380 | 1.0 |
| 2 | 0x14465f917 | frontfin_fr | centre 0x144660380 | 0.6 |
| 3 | 0x14465f991 | frontfin_fl | centre 0x144660380 | 0.75 |
| 4 | 0x14465fa12 | frontwing_fr_01 | right 0x1446603d0 | 0.25 |
| 5 | 0x14465fa8c | frontwing_fr_02 | right 0x1446603d0 | 0.5 |
| 6 | 0x14465fb06 | frontflap_fr | right 0x1446603d0 | 0.75 |
| 7 | 0x14465fb87 | frontwing_fl_01 | left 0x1446603a0 | 0.25 |
| 8 | 0x14465fc01 | frontwing_fl_02 | left 0x1446603a0 | 0.5 |
| 9 | 0x14465fc7b | frontflap_fl | left 0x1446603a0 | 0.75 |
| 10 | 0x14465fcec | frontwingmoving_fl | left 0x1446603a0 | 0.5 |
| 11 | 0x14465fd5d | frontwingmoving_fr | right 0x1446603d0 | 0.5 |

`Car_Register_DetachablePart_Thunk` is a jmp with no `.pdata` entry, so a `func_start` trace there is refused. The trace hooks `Car_Create_DetachablePart` instead, with a 3-frame backtrace through the `.data1` bodies (both have `.pdata`). The first frame (= ret) is 0x15538e511 in the add body. The second is 0x15537d5ee for a main piece or 0x15537d740 for a sub-piece. The third is the Init return in the table. A later Init on a car whose list is already filled registers nothing. Calls from 0x144655640 (return 0x144655961, the second part source) are not part of the question.

## Expected log lines
Addresses in `ret`/`bt` are rebased. Field values (`pred`, `ctx`) are raw: subtract the `H` start line's `image_base` and add 0x140000000 before comparing.

- `R .* hook=t130\.wing_init .*status=installed` and `R .* hook=t130\.create .*status=installed` → both hooks resolved.
- `E .* hook=t130\.wing_init .* rdx=0x(0|[0-9a-f]*00) .* vid=(0x[0-9a-f]+) .* ret=0x144629740` with `parts_begin` == `parts_end` → the build-time Init, once per car, with an empty list.
- `E .* hook=t130\.create .* vid=(0x[0-9a-f]+) .* pred=(0x[0-9a-f]+) ctx=(0x[0-9a-f]+) .* k=([0-9.]+) .* bt=0x15538e511,0x15537d5ee,(0x14465f[0-9a-f]+)` → one main piece, with the third bt frame naming the Init call site.
- `E .* hook=t130\.wing_init .* ret=0x1555bdfed` with `parts_begin` != `parts_end` → a later Init (reset path) on a filled list.

Pass rule:

- Every vehicle id with a build-time `t130.wing_init` has exactly 11 main-piece `t130.create` events (second bt frame 0x15537d5ee, third frame in the table).
- Each of the 11 Init returns appears once per vehicle, with that row's predicate (rebased) and k (within 1e-6), and ctx equal to that vehicle's `t130.wing_init` rcx.
- No `t130.create` event whose third bt frame is an Init return follows a `t130.wing_init` with `parts_begin` != `parts_end`.

Fail rule: any of these is broken, for example a different count, predicate, k or ctx, or pieces registered on a later Init. Sub-piece events (second frame 0x15537d740) and 0x144655961 events are logged but do not count. If no later Init is seen (step 3 did not reset the cars), the third clause is unanswered: report the run as a pass of the first two clauses only.
