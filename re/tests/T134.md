## Question
Does Game_CareerSetup_ResetEntrants run only when a career is created, never at Start Next Season or at a career load?

## Hypothesis
`Game_CareerSetup_ResetEntrants` (0x140c34960, CareerSetupComponent slot 9) and `Game_CareerSetup_BuildEntrants` (0x140c2a870, front-end callers only) copy the data group's entrant list into CareerEntity +0x2d8 when a career is made. Nothing re-copies it at a season start: the stock game keeps one team set for a whole career, and driver moves rewrite single rows through `Career_Set_EntrantData_Thunk`. So a core that changes a season's teams writes the career's rows itself.

## Promotes
- `Game_CareerSetup_ResetEntrants`: L2 -> L3; `Game_CareerSetup_BuildEntrants`: L1 -> L2 if its hits match the front-end callers.
- A hit at Start Next Season or at a load demotes the "participants are fixed per career" claim in work/std16/RE-CAREER-TEAMS.md and moves `career::apply_teams` to after that call.
