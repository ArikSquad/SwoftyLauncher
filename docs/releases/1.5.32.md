# 1.5.32

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.32 names an outdated F1 25 as the cause of a crash and logs each step of the launcher's startup.

## Launcher

- When F1 25 crashes on a build older than 1.0.141.7865 and nothing else explains the crash, the crash screen and **Send Logs** say to update F1 25 in Steam or the EA app.
- The launcher writes each step of its startup to `launcher-app.log`. When no window has appeared after 10 seconds, it writes the last step it reached, every 10 seconds for a minute.
