# 1.2.1

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.2.1 adds a line to the offline play terms: we may collect anonymised usage data from your use of the launcher. The launcher asks you to accept the terms again before your next modded session.

Download it from [Releases](https://github.com/Swofty-Developments/SwoftyLauncher/releases) or with the [installer](/guide/installation). The launcher offers each new release itself.
