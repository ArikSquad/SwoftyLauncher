# 1.5.21

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.21 says more about why two copies of an addon differ.

## Launcher

- When the launcher finds that your copy of an addon differs from another player's, it names the file or the part of the build that differs, and **Send Logs** includes both copies' details.
