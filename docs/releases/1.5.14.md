# 1.5.14

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.14 fixes the Discord **Log in** button on Windows.

## Launcher

- On Windows, a left click on **Log in** or **Log out** at the top right of the launcher signs you in or out. Before, it did nothing.
