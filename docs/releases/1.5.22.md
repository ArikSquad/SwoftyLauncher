# 1.5.22

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.22 fixes a Time Trial crash with Classic Seasons, and Progressive Career no longer crashes when you finish a season.

## In game

- A Time Trial started after going back from team select to the season boxes loads only the Classic Seasons year you picked. It used to load every year at once, which ran F1 25 out of engine sound memory and closed it while the session loaded.

## Addons

- **Progressive Career** 1.2.3:
  - Finishing a season no longer crashes when your teammate leaves the team, such as Rosberg after 2016. The new season's line-ups arrive at the first Team HQ after the calendar screens.
  - With **Follow your driver's real career**, the next season's contract no longer crashes the game at the start of a season. It's signed at that same Team HQ, still with no contract event and no contract scene.
