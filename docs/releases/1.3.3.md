# 1.3.3

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.3.3 names Team Simplified's importer the Simplified Importer and lets you drag addons on and off.

## Simplified Importer

- The Mod Importer is now the **Simplified Importer**, under its own **SIMPLIFIED FOR SWOFTY** group in the sidebar. See [Simplified Importer](/using/mods).
- Addons made from mods show **Simplified** as their source.
- The addon panel no longer has **Open in Mod Importer**.

## Addons page

- The list has **On** and **Off** groups. Drag a row into the other group to turn it on or off, or use its switch. Dropped on a row that is on, an addon turns on in that place in the load order. See [Turn addons on or off](/using/addons#turn-addons-on-or-off).
- SwoftyMod and Simplified for Swofty always load first: nothing can be dragged above them.
- Track addons without an icon of their own show a race track.
