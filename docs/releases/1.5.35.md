# 1.5.35

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.35 makes restoring a profile save safe.

## Launcher

- **Restore** on a save notice, and `swofty saves restore`, wait until F1 25 and Steam are closed. Restoring while Steam ran could make F1 25 call the restored save corrupted, and accepting its recovery reset settings and setups. Exit Steam completely (Steam menu > Exit), restore, then start Steam, which uploads the restored save.
- [Profiles and restoring](/using/profiles-and-restoring#restoring-a-profile-save) shows how to get a profile back after accepting F1 25's recovery.
