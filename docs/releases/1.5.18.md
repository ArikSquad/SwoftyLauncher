# 1.5.18

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.18 says which F1 25 update to install when an addon needs a newer game, and Progressive Career no longer crashes when you start a new career and races the seasons before 2026 on the right fuel.

## Launcher

- When an addon's changes don't apply because F1 25 is older than its latest update, Play names the addon, your F1 25 version and the update it needs, instead of showing an error from the game's files. See [Windows](/guide/windows) and [Linux](/guide/linux).

## Addons

- **Progressive Career** 1.2.2:
  - Starting a new career with **Follow your driver's real career** no longer crashes on Confirm. Your driver signs with his real team at the first Team HQ, still with no contract event and no contract scene.
  - Seasons before 2026 start races with the 2025 fuel loads, for you and the AI, instead of the 2026 ones, which left the older cars about a quarter short. 2026 seasons keep the 2026 fuel.
