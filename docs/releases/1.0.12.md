# 1.0.12

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.0.12 adds **Send Logs to Developers** at the bottom of the sidebar. It asks for your Discord username and what went wrong, then sends your launcher and game logs to the developers. Passwords and tokens are left out. See [Send your logs to the developers](/using/send-logs).

The [Driver Manager](/using/driver-manager) addon needs this release. Its page creates new drivers: name, code, number, nationality, birth date, head, helmet, suit, gloves, pose, podium emote, commentary name, ratings and AI traits, with previews of each look taken from your game. New drivers are free agents in careers you start after adding them.

The [Career Mode Editor](/using/career-editor) 0.2.0 lists the calendar with each round's track, moves rounds with a preview of the result, and adds or replaces rounds with any track in your profile, installed modded tracks included. Replacing a round in a Driver Career no longer fails with `bad_args`. The editor's page also responds when the profile loads nothing at the early stage.

Turning off or removing an addon that marks itself as affecting saves now asks first, on the Addons page and in `swofty addons disable` and `swofty addons remove`. Offline play uses the extension's 0.3.9 release, whose Linux tools start on every distribution.

For addon authors:

- A form select can be searched with `search: true`, group its options under `{ heading }` titles and show an `image` beside each option, and an `image` block shows a picture from your plugin's folders. Long selects scroll. See [Launcher API](/reference/addons/launcher-api).
- A build reply can name the fields its `db.add-row` ops copy, and the `profile_tracks` host call lists the tracks in the profile's last build.
- `saves.affects` in the manifest marks an addon as affecting saves. See [Manifest](/reference/addons/manifest).
- swofty_core accepts event subscriptions and frame callbacks made before its events setup and arms them when the setup completes.

Download it from [Releases](https://github.com/Swofty-Developments/SwoftyLauncher/releases) or with the [installer](/guide/installation). The launcher offers each new release itself.
