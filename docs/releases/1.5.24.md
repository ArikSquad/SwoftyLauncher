# 1.5.24

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.24 explains more problems in plain words.

## Crash explanations

- When F1 25 stops with its own **Insufficient Storage** message, the launcher says how much space to free and on which drive. F1 25 checks drive C even when the game is installed on another drive.
- A crash from **Manual Pit Lane** during Return to Garage or a flashback in the pit lane names the addon and says to turn it off or avoid those moments.

## Launcher

- When an antivirus has removed the launcher's `swofty.exe`, the message says so, and how to restore it or reinstall.
- **Restart as administrator** comes back with the reason when the elevated copy closes straight away, instead of quitting silently. The launcher keeps its own log, which **Send Logs** includes.

## Addons

- **Classic Seasons** F1 2016, F1 2017 and F1 2018 0.6.5 (already out): the engine sound no longer drops or cuts out on gear changes.
