# 1.5.6

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.6 makes the installer open Swofty Launcher without administrator rights, and a failed launch now says what went wrong first. Career Mode Editor 0.3.1 gives a free agent a race number when he takes a seat.

## Installing

- The installer runs without administrator rights and opens Swofty Launcher without them, and without the installer's Windows compatibility settings. Before, a launcher the installer opened could run as administrator, and F1 25 started from the EA app couldn't write in its own folder.

## Launching

- A failed launch's notice starts with what went wrong, such as a blocked download, and says what the launcher was doing in brackets. The tooltip and the log keep the full text.

## Addons

- [Career Mode Editor](/using/career-editor) 0.3.1 needs 1.5.6. A free agent who takes a seat in a swap gets a race number no other driver holds, the way the game numbers its own signings. Undoing the swap takes the number away again.
- Classic Seasons 0.5.1 labels the real driver heads' F1 25 materials as F1 25 materials. Grand Prix sessions with those drivers crashed now and then while loading.
