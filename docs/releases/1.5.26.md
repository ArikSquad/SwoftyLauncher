# 1.5.26

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.26 recovers the EA app copy after the EA app repairs it, and gives addons 43 more game functions.

## Launcher

- On the EA app copy, when the EA app's repair replaces offline play's file, the launcher puts it back as F1 25 starts and says to close F1 25 and press Play again. A restore keeps the EA app's newer anti-cheat file and never puts offline play's file back as EA's.

## For addon makers

- 43 more game functions are mapped for native addons: race start, the pit lane, red flags, the safety car, penalties, weather and parts detach. They come from vogtmarcel's research.
