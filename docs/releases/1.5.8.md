# 1.5.8

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.8 gives addon pages richer tables. TeamSimplified contributed the change.

## Addons

- A table on an addon's page can show a picture in a cell, such as a driver's portrait or a flag, in taller rows.
- A cell can be a switch that turns something on or off in place.
- Part of a cell's text can be bold or coloured, such as a driver's surname.
- Clicking a row can open a panel with more about it beside the table, as a row of the DB Editor does. In a narrow window the panel opens under the row.
- Addon authors: see [Tables](/reference/addons/launcher-api#tables) and [Row details](/reference/addons/launcher-api#row-details). An addon that uses them sets `"compat": { "launcher": ">=1.5.8" }`.
