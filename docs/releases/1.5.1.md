# 1.5.1

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.1 downloads larger addons from the Addon Browser. Addons up to 4 GB install, such as the Classic Seasons seasons, and downloads come from Swofty's CDN at `cdn.swofty.net`.

## Addon Browser downloads

- The Addon Browser installs addons up to 4 GB. Earlier launchers stop at 300 MB, so an addon over that size needs 1.5.1.
- Downloads follow the registry to Swofty's CDN. The launcher still checks every download's size and SHA-256 against the Addon Browser's listing before it installs.
- A download gets as long as its size needs: at least 30 minutes, and longer for large files on a slow connection. Connecting and the first response still time out quickly when the registry or CDN can't be reached.
- A download that runs past its listed size, or whose server reports a different size, stops with an error instead of installing.
