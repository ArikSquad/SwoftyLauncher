# 1.5.10

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.5.10 gives addon careers their own row on the career select.

## Career

- Career > Driver Career and Career > My Team Career list the 2025 and 2026 careers under **Built In** and addon careers, such as the 1990 season, under **Addons**. Up and Down move between the rows, and the description below follows the selected career. With no addon career for that mode, the screen is the stock one.
- Addon authors: a career content shows in Driver Career, My Team or both. See [Career contents](/reference/std/career-content). An addon that registers one for My Team sets `"compat": { "launcher": ">=1.5.10" }`.

## Addons

- An addon that's on while an addon it requires is missing, off or at a version outside its range shows **Needs** with that addon's name on the Addons page. Play used to fail with no sign there.
- An addon listed above one it requires shows **Loads after**. The build loads the required addon first.
- The **Requires** line in an addon's panel says which requirement is not installed, off, at another version or loads first. See [requires](/reference/addons/manifest#requires-conflicts-load-after).

## Tracks

- Overtake mode works on community tracks in season cars that addons put as whole team archives, such as Classic Seasons 0.5.1. Those archives went into the game without the tracks' overtake points. See [ERS](/reference/installing-tracks#ers).
