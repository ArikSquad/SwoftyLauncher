# Swofty Launcher for F1 25

With **Swofty Launcher for F1 25**, you can install addons, mods and community tracks for [F1 25](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3059520/), group them into profiles, and launch the game with them. You can use it on Windows or on Linux through Steam and Proton, including the Steam Deck. Modded play is single-player and offline.

These pages cover playing with the launcher. To make your own addons, mods or tracks, see the [Developer overview](/reference/).

## Get started

1. **Install the launcher** with the installer or by hand: [Installation](/guide/installation).
2. **Set it up** to find F1 25 on [Windows](/guide/windows), or add the Steam launch option on [Linux and Steam Deck](/guide/linux).
3. **Add content**: addons on the [Addons](/using/addons) page, mods and tracks on the [Mods](/using/mods) page. [Addons, mods and tracks](/guide/addons-mods-tracks) explains which is which.
4. **Pick a profile and press Play**: choose your setup on the [Profiles](/using/profiles) page, then change addon settings in game under [ADDON SETTINGS](/using/swofty-mod).
5. **After playing**, the game folder rolls back to vanilla on its own; [restore the game](/using/profiles-and-restoring) or [uninstall](/guide/uninstall) the launcher when you need to.
